# Freeze

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 08

See spec, "Live control".

## Acceptance

- A Freeze toggle on the Fallback Panel, with a keyboard shortcut.
- While on, every movement Effect holds its current offset. Directions, fades and other attributes still apply.
- Turning it off resumes the Effects in phase with the beat (they jump to where they would be).
- Not saved. Off on launch.
- Tests: held offset while on, Direction change while frozen, resume.
